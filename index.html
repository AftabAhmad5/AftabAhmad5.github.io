Off-grid solar–wind–battery–diesel feasibility study

Rural electrification, Kampung Bario, Sarawak, Malaysia

  • Power & renewables
46.9%lower 25-year cost than diesel-only
60.7%less diesel burned per year
  • Modelled a standalone hybrid system in HOMER Pro for a representative 500 kWh/day load (60 kW peak), using more than 40 years of NASA POWER solar and wind data.
  • The best configuration is 75 kW of PV, 200 kWh of battery, a 67 kW diesel generator and a 34 kW converter. It serves 100% of the load with a 41% renewable fraction, a net present cost of $446k against $841k for diesel-only, an LCOE of $0.19 against $0.36/kWh, and a 2.9-year simple payback.
  • Ran a 27-case sensitivity study across diesel price, solar irradiance and wind speed. The hybrid was cheaper than diesel-only in every case (37% to 64% savings) with 0% unmet load throughout.
  • Showed that the site's weak wind (about 1.2 m/s at 50 m) does not justify a turbine, and documented the with-wind option separately for comparison. Listed the assumptions and search-space limits to validate before procurement.
  • HOMER Pro
  • NASA POWER data
  • solar PV
  • battery storage
  • diesel generator
  • NPC/LCOE
